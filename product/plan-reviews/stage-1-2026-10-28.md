## Plan review: product/plans/stage-1.md

Reviewed while the plan sat in the private workspace; moved here with it on 2026-10-28, with
business-plan budgets, dates and go-to-market quotes removed. Finding numbers and phase numbers
are as reviewed (the plan's phases were renumbered when finding 17 added a phase).

**Verdict:** Revise first
**Lenses:** Scope, Engineering, Developer experience. Skipped Design: no user interface (a library and CLI, covered by Developer experience).

The plan takes its scope from `BUSINESS_PLAN.md` §05 Stage 1 item by item, keeps the default-no and Stage 2 items out per both rule files, and fits the business plan's Stage 1 budget. The four Open assumptions were reviewed as written and are not counted as blocking. Two findings block: the tripwire has no defined way to accept an intended server change, and one In item is marked "keep" but v0.4.0 does not have it.

## Questions for the owner

- When the tripwire goes red on an intended upgrade, how does the maintainer accept it? Recommended: `check --record` overwrites a check-written cassette only, and exits `2` on a full fixture session (finding 1).
- Should `check` use the existing record-mode words instead of its own `--record` flag (`once` records when absent, `none` refuses, `all` re-baselines)? Recommended: keep the explicit `--record`. A tripwire that quietly writes its own baseline in a CI that forgot `MCP_CASSETTE_MODE=none` would go green and guard nothing. Still use `resolve_mode()` for the `none` refusal (finding 5).
- Does `EVIDENCE.md` belong in the public repo when it links to the private suite's CI? Recommended: `.agents_workspace/product/EVIDENCE.md` (finding 18).

## Unverified

- Finding 7, the Windows `npx.cmd` process tree outliving a terminate: inferred from how cmd shims work, not reproduced here.
- Finding 6, servers stalling on an unanswered server request: general MCP server behaviour, not tested against a named third-party server.
- Finding 12, that a break from the stdin loop exits cleanly on every platform: inferred from the disconnect-fault path and its passing tests, not re-run.
